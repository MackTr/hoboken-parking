About this data
  • Curb schedules come from the city's street cleaning schedule, placed on the city's GIS street map. Where the two disagree, the schedule page wins and the curb's popup says so.
  • Street cleaning is suspended on city holidays.
  • One-way arrows come from OpenStreetMap, which is more current than the city's 2019 street map (it has Sinatra Drive's 2022 one-way change, for example).
  • Signs come from the city's temporary no-parking map.

Always check posted signs. Weather suspensions are announced on Nixle.
